Puis le prompt :

Photorealistic cinematic film still, same sequence and same character as the
reference shot, a few seconds later.

Manhattan sidewalk, midday, early April. The same young Korean man - black
leather biker jacket, black turtleneck, black tactical cargo pants, chunky
black sneakers, messy black hair - walks PAST the camera, crossing the frame.

SCREEN DIRECTION - this is essential and must be obeyed:
He is travelling from the LEFT side of the frame toward the RIGHT side.
His body is turned in three-quarter profile, shoulders angled to the right,
his path clearly heading right. He is NOT walking toward the camera and NOT
walking to the left. A viewer must instantly read "he is moving right".

Waist-up framing, seen from the side of the street.

Shot on an 85mm lens. Pedestrians pass in front of and behind him, layered
in depth, slightly out of focus. He is in shadow, the buildings behind are
sunlit. Cool grey, stone and pale blue palette.

2.39:1 anamorphic, natural light, no colour grade, no lens flare.
His face must be IDENTICAL to the reference shot - same actor, no drift.

INTERDIRE : different face, different actor, changed hairstyle, combat boots,
mask, glowing lights, night, warm sunset light, text overlay, watermark,
frontal walk toward camera, walking to the left, static standing pose.
