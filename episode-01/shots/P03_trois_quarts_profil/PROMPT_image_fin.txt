sait pas le faire apparaitre correctement sur une peau qui se decouvre en
cours de plan - il inventerait une tache. On le PEINT donc dans l'image de
fin, puis on interpole de l'image A vers l'image B.

REFERENCES A CHARGER (3, chacune pour un attribut different) :
  P03B_1_CADRAGE_fin.png       -> composition et angle (profil droit)
  P03B_2_CONTINUITE_debut_du_meme_plan.png -> le DEBUT DU MEME PLAN, deux
     secondes plus tot : meme homme, meme rue, meme lumiere, meme arriere-plan
  P03B_3_TATOUAGE_eteint.jpg   -> la marque : forme et emplacement exacts

Formuler ainsi :

@P03B_1_CADRAGE_fin is the exact framing and camera angle to reproduce.
@P03B_2_CONTINUITE is the SAME SHOT two seconds earlier - same man, same
street, same light, same background. This is the strongest constraint:
nothing may change except his body orientation. Keep his face identical.
@P03B_3_TATOUAGE shows the exact tattoo: shape, size and position on the
skin behind his right ear.

Puis le prompt :

Photorealistic cinematic film still: the SAME SHOT as the reference, two
seconds later. Same character, same street, same light, same background.
Only his orientation has changed.

Manhattan sidewalk, midday, early April. The same young Korean man - black
leather biker jacket, black turtleneck, black tactical cargo pants, chunky
black sneakers, messy black hair - now seen in clean RIGHT PROFILE as he
walks past the camera from left to right. Waist-up framing.

On the skin just behind his right ear, partly under his hair, there is a
small dark tattoo. It is the KOREAN HANGUL SYLLABLE  속  - one single Korean
character, exactly as shown in the tattoo reference image. It is NOT a star,
NOT an asterisk, NOT a cross, NOT an abstract symbol: it is the Korean
letter 속, with its three strokes (a consonant, a vowel, a bottom consonant).
Plain black ink on skin. NOT lit, NOT glowing, NOT red, NOT neon.
Small and discreet - noticed without being readable.

EXPOSURE AND GRADE - critical for editing:
Match the exposure, contrast, brightness and colour temperature of the
continuity reference EXACTLY. These two images are two seconds apart in the
same shot: they must cut together invisibly. Do not darken the image, do not
make it cooler or more contrasted than the reference.

Shot on an 85mm lens. Pedestrians pass in front and behind, layered, out of
focus. He is in shadow, the buildings behind are sunlit. Cool grey, stone
and pale blue palette.

2.39:1 anamorphic, natural light, no colour grade.
His face must be IDENTICAL to the reference shot - same actor, no drift.

INTERDIRE : glowing tattoo, red glow, neon, light emission, star tattoo,
asterisk, abstract symbol, latin letters, mask, different face, different
actor, combat boots, night, sunset, darker grade, cooler grade, heavy
contrast, text overlay, watermark, walking to the left, facing the camera.
