First frame to last frame. CAMERA MOVE (smooth, steadicam): as he drops, the camera
glides slowly BACKWARD along the street, one or two metres, to end on the wider framing of the last
frame. Same height. One continuous, fluid move, no cut.

From the first frame his mask is ACTIVE, its red LED lines glowing, eyes red. His clothes are still
dark.

(0 - 3 s) He spins around toward us in ONE superb, fluid, continuous, athletic movement that flows
straight down into the professional sprinter's "set" position of the last frame: hands to the
asphalt, back leg extending, hips rising.
WHILE HE TURNS, the energy spreads FROM THE MASK: red light runs down his neck, then in a fast cascade
along the thin red LED lines of his jacket, arms, pants, down to his sneakers - fully lit exactly as
he lands in position.
(3 - 7 s) He holds the "set" position, perfectly still, coiled, eyes locked just past the lens.

The crowd on the right, already staring at him, JUMPS back, startled, gasping; they point, turn to
each other, cover their mouths, some film him. The street stays alive: passers-by, distant traffic,
never a frozen crowd.

SOUND: a whoosh of the fast turn, a crackling electric surge running from his head down his body as
the LEDs ignite; the crowd gasping, startled cries, amazed murmurs. No music.

Clean, sharp image, realistic. Real-time speed.

DO NOT: unlit mask, unmasked face, motorcycle, glowing aura, sparks, explosion, running, pausing
mid-turn, slow motion, jump cut, grain, text, watermark, music.
